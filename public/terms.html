1. What TeeRadar does

TeeRadar is a golf tee-time discovery and booking platform that helps golfers find available tee times and book rounds at participating golf courses.

Depending on the course, TeeRadar may:

  • Direct you to a third-party booking system operated by the golf course
  • Allow you to book tee times directly through the TeeRadar platform

TeeRadar operates as a technology platform connecting golfers with golf courses. TeeRadar does not own or operate the golf courses listed on the platform.

2. Tee time bookings through TeeRadar

Some courses allow tee times to be booked directly through TeeRadar. When you make a booking through the platform:

  • Your booking is submitted to the participating golf course.
  • The golf course remains responsible for the operation of the course, tee time availability, pricing and course rules.
  • TeeRadar acts only as the booking platform facilitating the reservation.

Once a booking is confirmed, the tee time is reserved subject to the golf course’s policies and conditions of play.

3. Payments

Some bookings may require payment at the time of booking through the TeeRadar platform. Payments are processed securely through a third-party payment provider.

TeeRadar does not store or process credit card information directly.

Where payment is required:

  • The total price will be shown before confirming the booking.
  • Payments may include green fees and optional add-ons such as carts or hire clubs.
  • Payment processing fees may be included in the total shown.

4. Pay at course bookings

Some courses allow bookings to be made online but paid directly at the golf course.

For these bookings:

  • The golf course is responsible for collecting payment.
  • The course may apply its own cancellation or payment policies.

5. Third-party booking systems

In some cases TeeRadar may redirect you to external booking systems such as MiClub, Quick18 or other course-managed platforms.

When you leave TeeRadar and enter a third-party booking page:

  • The course or third-party provider controls availability and pricing.
  • TeeRadar is not responsible for bookings made on those external systems.

6. Accuracy of information

TeeRadar attempts to provide accurate tee time availability and course information, however this information may change without notice.

The golf course remains the source of truth for tee time availability, pricing, course rules and operating conditions.

7. Cancellations and refunds

Cancellation policies may vary between golf courses.

If a booking is cancelled:

  • The golf course’s cancellation policy will apply.
  • Refund eligibility may depend on the course’s rules and the timing of the cancellation.

TeeRadar may assist in processing refunds where applicable but does not guarantee refunds unless required by law.
