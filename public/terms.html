1. About TeeRadar

TeeRadar is an online golf tee-time discovery and booking platform that allows golfers to find available tee times, receive availability alerts and make reservations at participating golf courses.

TeeRadar operates as a technology platform connecting golfers with golf courses. TeeRadar does not own, operate or manage the golf courses listed on the platform.

The golf courses listed on TeeRadar remain fully responsible for course operations, pricing, availability, cancellations, and the delivery of golf services.

2. Tee Time Bookings

TeeRadar may allow users to:

  • Book tee times directly through the TeeRadar booking platform
  • Be redirected to third-party booking systems
  • View aggregated tee time availability from multiple providers

When a booking is made through TeeRadar, the reservation request is transmitted to the participating golf course.

The golf course is responsible for:

  • Managing tee time availability
  • Course operations
  • Player limits and rules
  • Providing the golf round or services booked

TeeRadar does not guarantee the availability of specific tee times.
