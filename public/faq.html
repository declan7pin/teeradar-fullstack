Frequently Asked Questions

Do I book on TeeRadar?

Usually no — TeeRadar helps you find suitable tee times and then sends you directly to the course’s official booking page. This ensures pricing, availability, carts and conditions are always accurate and controlled by the course.

Can I track my scores on TeeRadar?

Yes — TeeRadar now includes built-in scorecards. You can create and save full scorecards, track strokes + putts per hole, and view your performance in My Stats over time.

Why do I have to log in?

Logging in helps us prevent abuse of course availability data and allows us to personalise your experience. It lets us save your preferences, favourite courses, and deliver alerts — as well as offer ad-free and premium features to subscribers.

Why does it sometimes say available when the course page looks different?

We read publicly available timesheets from platforms like MiClub and Quick18. If a course changes its layout, adds reCAPTCHA, or restricts access, we may only be able to identify that availability exists rather than confirm the exact slot. In those cases, clicking through to the course page is the final source of truth.

Which courses are included?

TeeRadar focuses on public golf courses across Australia. We launched with Western Australia — including courses like Araluen, Collier Park, Whaleback, The Cut, Secret Harbour, Sun City and others — and are actively expanding nationwide as more booking platforms are integrated.

Does TeeRadar support all of Australia?

Yes. TeeRadar is built to operate nationally. As usage grows, we’re able to request official API access from course platforms, which allows faster, more reliable and real-time availability across all Australian states.

Why do some courses show a phone number instead of a booking link?

Some public courses across Australia only accept bookings by phone. We still include these courses so golfers know they exist, and we show the phone number directly to make booking as easy as possible.

What do I get with a TeeRadar subscription?

Subscribing unlocks features designed to save you time and help you secure the tee times you actually want. This includes instant alerts when new tee times become available at your favourite courses, the ability to track multiple courses, fewer ads, and priority access to new features as TeeRadar expands across Australia.

Does TeeRadar charge me?

TeeRadar currently offers free access while we grow. Optional subscriptions are available for golfers who want real-time alerts, extra features and a cleaner, ad-free experience. Subscriptions help fund infrastructure, data access and continued expansion.

I run a golf course — can TeeRadar power our bookings?

Yes — TeeRadar can provide a lightweight booking platform for public courses, including course admin tools, manual bookings, and booking analytics. If you’re interested in partnering with TeeRadar, email us at teeRadar.help@gmail.com.
