About TeeRadar

TeeRadar helps golfers find tee times across Australia faster — without bouncing between a dozen different course websites. Set your filters, see which courses match, then jump straight to the course’s official booking page to lock it in.

Why we built it

Booking golf can be messy: every course uses different booking providers, different layouts, and different ways of showing availability. TeeRadar brings public booking options into one clean experience so you can find what’s available and book with less effort.

How it works

You choose the date, time window, number of players, and 9 or 18 holes. TeeRadar checks each course’s public booking page and highlights the ones worth clicking into. When you select a course, we send you directly to the course’s own booking system to complete the booking.

That means pricing, carts, policies, and final availability always come from the course site — you stay compliant and the course stays in control.

What you get with a subscription

Subscribers unlock features designed to help you win the tee times you actually want:

  • Instant tee time alerts when new times open up at your favourite courses
  • Track favourites so you don’t have to re-search every time
  • Priority access to new features as we roll them out

What’s next

We’re live across Australia and continuing to expand coverage and integrations (MiClub, Quick18 and more). As more courses and platforms support cleaner access, the experience becomes even more real-time and complete.
