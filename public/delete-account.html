Delete your TeeRadar account

TeeRadar users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

Delete your account from TeeRadar

  1. Open TeeRadar and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Scroll to the Delete Account section.
  4. Select Delete Account and follow the prompts.

If you cannot access your account

If you are unable to access your TeeRadar account, you can contact TeeRadar support from the email address associated with your account and request that your account be deleted.

What data is deleted?

When your account is deleted, your TeeRadar account and associated personal data are deleted, including account information and data associated with your use of TeeRadar.

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory purposes.
