Privacy & Policies

Last updated: 21 June 2026
This page explains how TeeRadar collects, uses and protects information across our website and mobile application.

1. Who we are

TeeRadar is a golf tee-time search, alert and scorecard platform based in Western Australia. TeeRadar helps golfers discover available tee times, save favourite courses, receive alerts, record rounds, track golf statistics and connect with friends.

2. What TeeRadar does

TeeRadar lets users search Australian golf courses by date, time window, holes and number of players. When you click a booking result, you are generally taken to the golf course’s official booking platform or website, such as MiClub, Quick18 or the club’s own website.

Any golf booking you make is between you and the relevant golf course or booking provider. TeeRadar does not process golf course booking payments directly.

TeeRadar may offer paid subscription plans that provide access to additional features, alerts and app functionality. Subscription payments may be processed through Apple, Stripe or another authorised payment provider.

3. Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you use TeeRadar:

  • Account details: Your name, email address, encrypted password, home course, favourite courses, age, gender and profile information you choose to provide.
  • Golf activity: Saved rounds, scorecards, scores, player names, statistics, handicap-related information, upcoming rounds and golf preferences.
  • Friends and social features: Friend requests, accepted connections, shared rounds and information you choose to share with other TeeRadar users.
  • Subscription information: Subscription status, plan type, billing status, renewal status and entitlement information. TeeRadar does not store full payment card details.
  • Push notification data: Device identifiers and notification tokens required to deliver tee-time alerts, friend requests, shared round notifications and other app notifications.
  • Contact messages: If you contact us, we receive the details you submit so we can reply and provide support.
  • Usage data: Searches performed, pages viewed, buttons clicked, booking referrals, alert activity and general product usage.
  • Technical data: Device type, browser type, operating system, approximate location, IP address and similar technical information.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

TeeRadar and our partners may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your login session.
  • Remember preferences such as your last search date, favourite courses or home course.
  • Measure traffic and usage patterns.
  • Show and measure advertising on the website.
  • Support app and website functionality.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some features, such as staying logged in, may not work as expected.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

We may use Google AdSense to show ads on TeeRadar. This means:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and/or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to TeeRadar and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising from Google by visiting your Google Ads Settings page. You can also learn more at Google’s advertising privacy page.

We do not have access to raw data from Google’s advertising cookies. We only see aggregated reports, such as how many ad impressions were shown.

6. How we use your information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain TeeRadar services.
  • Operate your account and remember your preferences.
  • Manage subscriptions and feature access.
  • Deliver tee-time alerts, email alerts and push notifications.
  • Store scorecards, rounds and golf statistics.
  • Operate friend, shared-round and social features.
  • Respond to support or contact requests.
  • Monitor usage, detect abuse and keep the service secure.
  • Improve TeeRadar features, layouts, alerts and course coverage.
  • Show, measure and improve advertising where applicable.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

7. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers: Companies that help us host the site, store our database, process analytics, send emails, deliver push notifications, manage payments or operate app services.
  • Payment providers: Apple, Stripe or other authorised providers may process subscription payments. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.
  • Other TeeRadar users: Information you choose to share through friend features, shared rounds, player names or social features may be visible to those users.
  • Legal or safety purposes: If required by law, or if needed to protect the rights, property or safety of TeeRadar, our users or others.

8. Links to course and third-party sites

When you click a booking link, you may leave TeeRadar and go to a golf course’s own website or booking platform. Their privacy policies and terms apply there, not ours. We do not control those systems and do not receive your golf booking payment details.

9. Data storage and security

We use reputable third-party hosting, database, analytics, notification and payment providers to operate TeeRadar.

Reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards are used to protect information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. Passwords are stored using encryption and secure authentication practices.

While we take reasonable precautions, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. How long we keep information

We keep account, subscription, alert, scorecard, friend and analytics data only for as long as we need it for the purposes described above, or as required by law.

We may anonymise or aggregate data so it can no longer be linked to you.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your account and associated personal information, where legally allowed.
  • Object to certain uses of your data, such as direct marketing.
  • Disable push notifications through your device settings.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through the relevant payment provider, such as Apple or Stripe.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

12. Account deletion

Users may request deletion of their TeeRadar account and associated personal information at any time.

Account deletion requests can be submitted through the TeeRadar application where available, or by contacting us at:

Email: teeradar.help@gmail.com

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymise personal information unless we are required to retain certain records for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting or operational purposes.

13. Children

TeeRadar is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

14. Contact us
